Methodology

The Monday script searches OpenAlex, Crossref, Europe PMC, and preprint records using editable topic queries. A mosquito genus alone is not enough: papers must match Culex oviposition, urban-rural mosquito comparisons, European mosquito life history, aquatic invertebrate biocontrol, transferable aquatic behaviour, context-dependent biological control, eDNA, or the strict European WNV/SINV watch.

It accepts only DOI-identified records with an earliest verified online-publication date inside the preceding seven days, rejects placeholders and configured exclusions, protects the existing archive when all sources fail, and deduplicates permanently by DOI first and normalized title second.

Preprints are labelled separately. When a preprint later appears as a journal article, matching DOI, title, and preprint metadata are linked so the article is not counted as an entirely new study.
